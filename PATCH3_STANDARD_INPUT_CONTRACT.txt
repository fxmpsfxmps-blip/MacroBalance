PATCH3 STANDARD INPUT CONTRACT
==============================

Source: frozen PATCH3 candidate
MB_CANDIDATE_V1_PATCH3_L005_INPUT_CONTRACT_STABILIZATION_2026-08-28.

Required for every active Standard run
---------------------------------------
sex: MALE or FEMALE. Required by normalization and A1 equation selection.
age: finite adult value accepted by the candidate (19+).
heightCm: finite positive number.
weightKg: finite positive number.
goal: LOSS, MAINTENANCE, or GAIN.
occupation: mapped movement context; current UI supplies one of SEATED,
STANDING_WALKING, PHYSICAL.
steps: finite non-negative number for the Standard activity evidence form.
stepsRepresentativeness: named evidence state: VARIABLE or MOSTLY_CONSISTENT.
VARIABLE means degraded representativeness evidence; MOSTLY_CONSISTENT means
usable representativeness evidence. Neither name is a confidence percentage,
probability, or TEE multiplier. Historical stepsCoverage=0.7 maps explicitly
to VARIABLE and historical stepsCoverage=0.9 maps explicitly to
MOSTLY_CONSISTENT. New active writes use stepsRepresentativeness; the numeric
field is a read-only compatibility input for old saved profiles/fixtures.
stepsObservationDays: positive observation window.

Activity-evidence heuristics
----------------------------
Seven observed days is a PRODUCT_DEFAULT / DEFENSIBLE_HEURISTIC, not a
biological boundary. Step bands are PRODUCT HEURISTICS pending future
calibration. The B1 relative TEE width <=25% rule is a PRODUCT UNCERTAINTY
ROUTING HEURISTIC, not a biological threshold. Their behavior is unchanged by
ACTIVITY_INPUT_POLICY_R1.

Training tuple
--------------
CONFIRMED_NONE is represented explicitly as trainingModality=NONE,
sessionsPerWeek=0, sessionDurationMin=0, workload=LOW, realized status.
Realized training requires modality, sessionsPerWeek > 0,
sessionDurationMin > 0, and broad workload. Missing any part remains
PARTIAL_OR_UNKNOWN_TRAINING; it is never coerced to NONE.
No estoy seguro sends UNKNOWN status with null tuple fields.
Planned/future training is not represented by this UI and is not inferred.

Optional/contextual
-------------------
bodyFatPercent is optional and may be null. The active adapter labels its source
and quality UNKNOWN; it does not invent DXA/BODPOD evidence.
sleepHours and stressLevel are optional context signals.
body composition method, EFA intake, clinical flags, rate point, diet pattern,
two-a-day, and longest session are not collected by the Standard UI; they remain
null/unknown and may route to a broader region or future Advanced flow.

Missingness semantics
---------------------
EMPTY and unknown training values remain distinct from explicit NONE.
No field is imputed to avoid a user question. If the required Standard activity
evidence is absent, the UI shows a short request-information message and does
not activate the legacy formula fallback.
