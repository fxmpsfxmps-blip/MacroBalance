STANDARD PROMOTION OLD VS NEW SMOKE (OBSERVATIONAL)
===================================================

The comparison is intentionally observational; numerical deltas are not failures.
Old values come from the legacy formula block preserved for rollback. New values
come from PATCH3's joint region through the compatibility adapter.

Profiles exercised locally:
- sedentary young male, explicit NONE
- sedentary young female, explicit NONE
- resistance-trained male, 4 x 60 min, MODERATE
- resistance-trained female, 4 x 60 min, MODERATE
- overweight/obesity with body fat unknown
- training UNKNOWN/PARTIAL
- weight-loss, maintenance, and gain intents

For each successful new run, kcal/P/C/F are finite and energyCheck is coherent
after display rounding. UNKNOWN/PARTIAL training remains labeled in the PATCH3
candidate output. The new route does not invoke the old standard formulas.

The female 55 kg row illustrates why deltas are diagnostic only: an envelope
selection can make the displayed deficit endpoint close to or above the selected
maintenance endpoint when uncertainty is wide. The adapter uses one common lower
energy endpoint per candidate run and discloses the underlying range; it does not
claim the old/new values are scientifically equivalent.

No physiological equivalence claim is made, and no old/new delta is used as a
promotion failure criterion.
