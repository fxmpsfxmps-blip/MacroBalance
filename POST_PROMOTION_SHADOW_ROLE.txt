POST-PROMOTION SHADOW ROLE
==========================

After PATCH3 becomes the active Standard producer, the existing Shadow client
remains observational only and is flag-off by default. It may be retained as
transition telemetry to compare the promoted contract and detect regressions;
it never supplies the active UI result, Firebase target, or menu authorization.
The next Candidate can later reuse the isolated Shadow endpoint. Shadow failures,
datastore issues, or disabled flags cannot prevent the active Standard adapter
from calculating.

RAW INPUT remains separate from normalized active input. Shadow capture now also
records the Standard activity/training fields, while the mapper preserves
explicit NONE versus UNKNOWN/PARTIAL semantics.
